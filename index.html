Owner, Brother Collection

I opened Brother Collection a year ago with a simple idea. People in Jarawan should not have to travel to the city just to get a good shirt or a well-fitting pair of jeans.

Many of our customers are young people and families from nearby villages. They want something that looks good and fits the budget. I speak to each of them myself and help pick the size and style.

The shop is clean, bright and neatly arranged, so you can see everything easily. Come in, take your time, and ask anything.
